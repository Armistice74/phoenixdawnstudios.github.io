5. Packs and purchases

The app is free to download. You can buy extra Crushes in Packs on the app's Packs tab. Each Pack has 5 Crushes, plus a Secret Crush that comes with the complete set. Every Pack purchase is a one-time in-app purchase through the Apple App Store.

Ways to collect a Pack

  • A draw gives you one Crush from that Pack that you don't have yet. You never get a repeat, so collecting a whole Pack by drawing always takes exactly 5 draws.
  • The whole pack gives you all 5 Crushes and the Secret Crush at once. Its contents are fixed and shown before you buy.
  • “The other N” (for example, “Get the other 3”) gives you every Crush in that Pack you don't have yet, plus the Secret Crush. Its contents are fixed too.
  • The Secret Crush is never sold on its own and never comes from a draw. You get them when you have all 5 of the Pack's Crushes, whichever way you collected them.

Odds and your draw order

  • Odds before every draw. Before you buy a draw, the app shows the odds of each outcome, along with the price.
  • Your order is fixed. The order in which draws give you a Pack's Crushes is shuffled once for each Apple Account, using an ID Apple gives your account for this app. It never changes after that. Every order is equally likely, so for a new draw, each Crush you don't have yet is equally likely.
  • What that means for you. Because the order is tied to your Apple Account, Restore Purchases gives you back exactly the same Crushes on any iPhone. It also means a refund never re-rolls a draw: if a draw is refunded and you draw again, you get the same Crush that refunded draw gave you. When that's the case, the app names that Crush and shows its odds as 100% before you buy, and asks you to confirm.

Prices and availability

  • Prices are set in the App Store and shown in the app in your local currency, including any tax Apple collects. They can differ between countries and can change.
  • What's for sale depends on your country. Some countries' App Stores (Japan, for example) sell only the whole pack, with no draws. The app shows only what your country's App Store sells.
  • If Apple tells the app you're 16 or 17 (outside Brazil, where the app is 18+), the app offers you only the whole pack and other purchases with fixed contents, not draws.

A license, not ownership

When you buy Crushes, you're getting a license to use them in Snooze Crush on the Apple Account that bought them, under these terms. You don't own them. They have no cash value, can't be exchanged for money, and can't be sold, traded, gifted, or transferred to anyone else. Getting what you bought

Refunds

If you no longer have all 5 of the Pack's Crushes, the Pack's Secret Crush is removed too. Any day you'd planned with a removed Crush goes back to May Day, the app's default Crush.
  • A refund doesn't re-roll a draw. If you draw again in that Pack after a refund, you get the same Crush as before, and the app tells you so before you buy (see Odds and your draw order). Please keep this in mind before you ask Apple for a refund on a draw.

Restoring purchases

The app checks your purchases with the App Store when it opens, so on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, your Crushes usually come back on their own. If they don't, tap Restore Purchases on the Packs tab or in Settings › Help, while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought them with. There's no separate account with us.
