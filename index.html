The studio

What we make

Phoenix Dawn Studios is an independent app studio. We make playful, polished mobile apps with real personality: everyday rituals, reimagined to make you smile. First up is a whole new way to wake up.

  • Characters with charm

    We build apps around memorable characters, real voices, and little moments of delight you'll actually look forward to.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your alarms and settings stay on your device, not on our servers.

  • Small studio, big care

    Being independent means every detail gets our full attention, from the first chime to the final polish.

Our anime alarm app

Wake up to a cast of collectible anime characters who greet you with short, voiced, animated wake-up clips. Collect them as trading cards, choose who wakes you on each day of the week, and turn your alarm into the best part of the morning. Playful and a little flirty, never explicit: made for grown-ups who would rather wake up smiling.

  • Collectible characters, each with their own trading card
  • Short voiced, animated wake-up clips
  • A different wake-up character for every day of the week
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking
