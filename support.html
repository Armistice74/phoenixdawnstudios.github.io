Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

Sorry about that. Nobody likes a missed morning. Most missed alarms come down to a phone setting. Here's what to check:

  • Ring in Silent mode: on iOS 26.2 and later, open Snooze Crush's Settings and check Ring in Silent mode. When it's on, your alarms ring like the Clock app's, even in Silent mode and Focus.
  • Permissions: go to your iPhone's Settings › Apps › Snooze Crush and make sure Notifications, and Alarms where it's listed, are allowed.
  • If alarms arrive as notifications (on iOS 26.0 and 26.1, or if you haven't allowed alarms): they don't make a sound in Silent mode, so keep Silent mode off overnight. To let them through a Focus like Sleep, go to Settings › Focus, choose your Focus, and allow Snooze Crush or its Time Sensitive notifications.
  • Stay up to date: keep both the app and iOS updated. We fix bugs and adapt to system changes as they come out.

Still stuck? Use Report a problem in the app's Settings, or email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the time the alarm was set for.

The alarm is too quiet (or too loud). How do I fix the volume?
  • The alarm sound: like Clock alarms, Snooze Crush's alarms follow your Ringtone and Alerts volume in Settings › Sounds & Haptics. (The side buttons change it only if Change with Buttons is on.)
  • Your Crush's call: the voice and video play at media volume. Turn it up with the side buttons while the call is on screen.
  • Headphones and speakers: if you're connected to Bluetooth, sound may play through that device. Disconnect before bed if you want the alarm to come from your iPhone.
How do Packs and draws work?

Packs are on the Packs tab. Each Pack has 5 Crushes, plus a Secret Crush you get with the complete set.

  • A draw gives you one Crush you don't have yet. You never get a repeat, so 5 draws always complete a Pack. The odds show right above the Draw button before you buy.
  • The whole pack, or “the other N” after one to three draws, gives you every Crush you're missing, plus the Secret Crush. No surprises.
  • In some countries (Japan, for example), the App Store sells only the whole pack.

When you buy Crushes, you get a license to use them in Snooze Crush on your Apple Account, not ownership. They have no cash value and can't be traded, gifted, or shared through Family Sharing. Our Terms of Use have the details.

Why did a draw give me the same Crush I had before?

The order your draws give Crushes in is shuffled once for your Apple Account and never changes. That's what lets Restore Purchases give you back exactly the same Crushes on any iPhone.

It also means that if a draw was refunded and you draw again, you get the same Crush that refunded draw gave you. There's no way to re-roll. When that's the case, the app names that Crush on the Pack's page, shows its odds as 100%, and asks you to confirm before you buy.

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone?

Usually you don't have to do anything: the app checks your purchases when it opens. If your Crushes don't show up, open the Packs tab and tap Restore Purchases (it's also in Settings › Help). Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy them, in Settings › your name › Media & Purchases. There's no separate account with us to create or remember.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue App Store refunds ourselves, and we never see your payment details.

If Apple refunds a purchase, the app removes what it gave you: the Crush from a refunded draw (or the Crushes from a refunded whole pack or “other N”), and the Pack's Secret Crush if you no longer have all 5. Days you'd planned with a removed Crush go back to May Day.

Good to know before you ask for a refund on a draw: a refund doesn't re-roll it. Your draw order is fixed for your Apple Account, so if you draw again in that Pack, you get the same Crush back. The app tells you which Crush it is before you buy.

If something went wrong with a purchase, like a Crush that didn't show up, let us know too. We're happy to help sort it out.

How do I delete my data?

Your alarms, Crush plans, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. To delete them, delete the app. (“Offload App” keeps your data, so choose “Delete App”.)

Our purchase service, RevenueCat, keeps a record of your purchases under a code made from your Apple Account. To have it deleted, email hello@phoenixdawnstudios.com with the Order ID from one of your Snooze Crush receipts. Deleting it doesn't remove your purchases from your Apple Account, and the app creates the record again the next time it opens on an iPhone signed in to that account. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Which devices are supported?

Snooze Crush is made for iPhone and needs iOS 26.0 or later. Alarms that ring through Silent mode and Focus need iOS 26.2 or later. Buying, restoring, and downloading Crushes need an internet connection; alarms don't. There's no Android version.

Who is the app for?

Snooze Crush is for ages 16 and up (18 and up on the Brazil App Store). If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to agree to our Terms of Use. The Crushes are playful and a little flirty, but never explicit, and all of them are fictional adults.

Do I need an account?

Nope. There's no sign-up, no login, and no password to forget. Purchases go through your Apple Account.

Can I use the app in Japanese?

Yes. Choose English or 日本語 in the app's Settings › Language, or let it follow your iPhone's language. In Japanese, the Crushes' lines are dubbed over the same animation, so the lips won't always match perfectly.

How were the Crushes made?

Every Crush is fully voiced and animated. Their voices, art, and video, and the app's music, were made with AI tools used under commercial licenses (ElevenLabs, Hedra, OpenAI, xAI's Grok, and Suno), then directed, selected, and edited by us.

How do I report a bug or a line that sounds wrong?

Thank you for helping us improve! In the app, go to Settings › Help and tap Report a problem or Suggest a fix to a line. Both open an email with the details we need already filled in. You can also flag a line during a call and send the report from the screen at the end of the call. If you'd rather write yourself, email us and include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the app version (Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Snooze Crush);
  • what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
  • steps to make it happen again, if you can;
  • for alarm issues: the time the alarm was set for and which day; and
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps show the problem.
