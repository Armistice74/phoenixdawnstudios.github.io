Conspiracy Tracker

A red-string evidence board in your pocket. Pin up photos, files, web links, notes and recordings, tie them together with string, and lay out the whole case on one board. Made for true-crime fans, podcast listeners and armchair detectives, and for any theory you can't stop thinking about.

  • Pin up photos, PDFs, web links, notes, video and audio
  • Tie anything to anything with colored string
  • Date your cards, and a timeline plays the case back in order
  • Lock a case behind Face ID or Touch ID
  • No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. We never see your cases.
  • Coming soon to the App Store

Free to start with one case. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited cases, with no subscription. Want a heads-up when it launches? Drop us a line.

How it works

Your case, on one board

Everything you've found goes up on the board. The strings show how it connects.

  • Pin up anything

    Photos from your library or camera, PDFs, web links with their preview, sticky notes, and video and audio clips. Theory, suspect and question cards keep your thinking in order, and you can make card formats of your own.

  • Tie it together

    Run string from card to card, color-code your threads, and tie a string to the exact words or the part of a photo that matters. Folders, layers and search keep a big case easy to follow.

  • Keep it to yourself

    No account and nothing to sign up for. Your cases stay on your device, and any case can lock behind Face ID or Touch ID. Save a backup file or a printable case file whenever you like.
