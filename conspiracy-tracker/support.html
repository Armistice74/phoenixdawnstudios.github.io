Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Tap Help on the shelf (next to the gear), or the ? on any board, and open Your first five minutes. When you make a case on the shelf, you can start from a blank board or a template: Cold case, Suspect board, Missing person, or Sugar Trails, a finished example board to try things on. Delete it whenever you want to start your own.

How do I tie a string?

Tap the String button on the left, then tap one thing and then another. The string appears between them. When you're done, tap Move. To change a string later, tap it: its box has the color, the line style and the name, and you can delete it there.

What's free, and what does the purchase unlock?

The free version gives you one case at a time, with one case folder in it. Sugar Trails counts as that case too. To start a different case, delete the one you have first: press and hold it on the shelf.

A one-time purchase through the App Store unlocks unlimited cases and folders. There's no subscription and there are no ads. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the shelf) and tap Restore purchases, or tap it on the unlock screen. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. There's no separate account with us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the purchase. If Apple refunds the unlock, the app goes back to the free limits. The cases you've already made aren't deleted.

Where are my cases kept? Can you see them?

Your cases, and everything you pin to them, are saved on your device, and, only if you turn on iCloud backup, in your own iCloud Drive. We can't see them, and there's no copy on our side, so we can't recover a case for you. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I back up my cases or move them to a new phone?

On the shelf, tap Save a backup file. It makes one file with every case in it. Keep it somewhere safe, like the Files app. On your new phone, tap Restore from a backup and choose that file.

A restore replaces what's on that phone. If you change your mind, Undo brings back what was there, until you close the app.

A backup has room for only so many photos, files and clips. If any didn't fit, the app tells you, and those cards come back without them. Anyone who has the backup file can open every case in it, including locked ones, so keep it private.

To have the app make backups by itself, turn on iCloud backup (see the next question).

How do I back up to iCloud, and restore on my other iPhone or iPad?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the shelf) and turn on Back up to iCloud Drive. It's off until you do. After you change something, the app saves a backup file in your own iCloud Drive by itself, at most once a day, usually when you leave the app. Back up to iCloud now makes one straight away. It keeps the two newest copies from each device, plus one at least a week old and one at least a month old once there are copies that old.

On your other iPhone or iPad, signed in to the same Apple Account, open Settings, tap Restore from iCloud, and choose a copy. Like any restore, it replaces what's on that device, and Undo brings back what was there, until you close the app. On a new device, iCloud may need a minute to catch up: if nothing's listed, try again.

Where the copies are: in the Files app, under iCloud Drive › Conspiracy Tracker. You can delete them there. Turning iCloud backup off stops new copies, but leaves the ones already there.

Locked cases are in the copies too, and anyone who can open your iCloud Drive can read them. We never see your backups.

How do I lock a case?

Tap Lock on the case's folder on the shelf. After that, your device's own Face ID or Touch ID (or its passcode) opens it, and the case locks again when you leave the app. Tap Locked to take the lock off. Your device needs Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode set up for the lock to work.

The app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode. A lock works inside the app only: backups, as a file or in iCloud Drive, have locked cases in them, and anyone who can open the backup can read them.

How do I share things into the app from Safari, Photos or other apps?

In the other app, tap its Share button and choose Conspiracy Tracker. Photos, videos, files, links and text all work. What you share lands in the Inbox (the Inbox button on any board's toolbar): drag it onto the board or tap Pin.

Don't see the app when you share? Scroll to the end of the row of apps and tap More: it's in the list there, and Edit can add it to the row.

How do I scan paper?

Tap + on the toolbar and choose Scan paper, then hold the camera over the page. It finds the edges and takes the page by itself. Add more pages, then tap Save. The pages become one card (up to 40 pages).

The app reads the words on your scans, and on the photos you pin up, right on your device, so you can search them, highlight them and tie strings to them. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Why don't I see Transcript on my video and audio clips?

Transcripts need iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. On earlier versions, the button doesn't show. To check yours, open the Settings app and tap General › About.

On iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, the app writes out what's said in a clip by itself, on your device, after you pin it to a board. Tap the clip, then Transcript, to read it, tap a line to jump there, or tag a moment. It listens for your device's main language. The first time, your device may need the internet to download Apple's speech model for it. The app never uses your microphone, and the audio never leaves your device.

I deleted something by mistake.

Tap Undo on the toolbar. It goes a long way back, until you close the app. A message with its own Undo button takes back just that one thing.

Can I share a case with someone?

Conspiracy Tracker has no accounts and no sharing between users, so your boards stay private. To show someone your work, take an evidence photo (the camera button in the top bar), or tap Print / save PDF to make a case file you can print, save or send.

Why does a web link show only its address?

When you add a link, the app asks that website for its title and preview picture. Some websites build their pages in a way the app can't read, or turn away apps. The card then shows just the address, and tapping Open on it still opens the page in your browser.

Who can use Conspiracy Tracker?

Anyone: there's no minimum age, and the app never stops anyone from using it. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to agree to our Terms of Use for you. Where the law calls for it, Apple asks a parent or guardian to approve a minor's downloads and purchases.
